## Direction contract

THESIS: Beyim Studio's interface is made of real, physical glass — panels of depth and light that teachers can trust the way they trust a well-made tool — refusing the flat, opaque Tailwind-admin-panel default this category always ships.

OWN-WORLD: Apple's Liquid Glass material language, translated to the web: translucent `backdrop-filter` panels with specular top edges, a near-white/light-gray neutral ground, the existing Beyim teal (#18999D) kept as the single committed accent riding on and through the glass (Restrained color strategy — neutrals plus one accent, appropriate for an Operate surface), Apple's system font stack (already configured: -apple-system/system-ui/Inter fallback) with size-specific tracking, generous continuous corner radii, and layered depth where structural chrome (sidebar, toolbars, sheets) sits as heavier glass above content that scrolls beneath it.

STORY: A teacher opens the app and immediately reads it as considered, physical, calm — navigation and surfaces that feel like glass catching light, not flat colored rectangles — while every control still behaves exactly as it did (same routes, same copy, fully bilingual kz/ru).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home hero keeps its teal gradient banner and typewriter headline; the glass card floating below it, the frosted sidebar rail beside it, and the pressable controls throughout now read as the Liquid Glass world rather than plain Tailwind white cards.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) — the user named the world directly: "Liquid Glass редизайн (рекомендую)" / "Применить актуальный визуальный язык Apple: стеклянные полупрозрачные поверхности, системная типографика, нейтральная палитра, характерные скругления и отступы — поверх уже сделанной физики анимаций."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
